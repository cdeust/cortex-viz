# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in cortex-viz a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, the
  claim, or the design, never the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn
  from the experience.
- Back technical claims with evidence: a reproduction, a measurement, a test,
  or a citation. "It works on my machine" is not an argument, and neither is
  seniority.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and commit messages) and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the
maintainer at **admin@ai-architect.tools**. Do not report it in a public issue.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

**Who enforces this.** cortex-viz has a single maintainer (see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)), so the maintainer both receives and decides
reports. That is stated openly because it is a real limitation: if your report
concerns the maintainer, you may instead report it to GitHub through
<https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>, which is outside this project's
control.

The maintainer may take any action deemed appropriate, including warning the
individual, temporarily or permanently banning them from interaction, editing
or removing comments, commits, and issues, and explaining the reason where
doing so does not compromise a reporter's privacy.
